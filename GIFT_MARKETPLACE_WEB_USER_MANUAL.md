## 1. Introduction to GIFT Marketplace Web

**GIFT Marketplace** is an all-in-one multi-module e-commerce web platform. Customers can conveniently buy everyday essentials, food, medicines, lifestyle products, send parcels, or book rental rides from a single unified web application.

---

## 2. Getting Started: Accessing the Web App & Setting Location

### Step 1: Open the Web Application
Open any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on your computer or mobile device and navigate to:  
`http://localhost:3000` (or your live website domain).

### Step 2: Set Your Delivery Location (Crucial First Step)
To see stores, products, and restaurants available in your area, set your location immediately upon landing:

1. Click the **"Select Location"** bar at the top-left of the page.
2. Allow browser location access for automatic GPS detection, OR type your City / Street address in the search box.
3. Click **Confirm Location**.

> 💡 **Tip:** Setting your exact location ensures that delivery times, delivery fees, and available stores are accurately calculated for your address.

---

## 3. Account Creation & Login Procedure

While users can browse products without signing in, creating an account is required to place orders, save delivery addresses, track live orders, and earn reward points.

### A. Creating a New Account (Sign Up)
1. Click the **"Sign In"** button at the top-right of the header.
2. Click **"Create Account"** or **"Sign Up"**.
3. Fill in your details:
   * **Full Name**
   * **Phone Number** (with country code selection)
   * **Email Address**
   * **Password** & **Confirm Password**
4. Check **"I agree to the Terms and Conditions"**.
5. Click **Sign Up**. Enter the One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your phone/email to verify.

### B. Logging In (Sign In)
1. Click **Sign In**.
2. Enter your registered Phone Number or Email and Password.
3. Click **Login**.

## 5. Searching Products, Stores & Categories

### Search Bar Usage
* Click the **Search Bar** located in the top navigation bar.
* Type the name of an item (e.g., *"Milk"*, *"Biryani"*, *"Panadol"*, *"Sneakers"*) or a store name.
* Press **Enter**. Filter results by Price, Popularity, Ratings, or Distance.

### Flash Sales & Campaigns
* **Flash Sales**: Click **Flash Sales** in the top navigation to view time-limited deep discounts.
* **Campaigns**: Browse seasonal deals, holiday sales, and special store promotions.
* **Brands**: Click **All Brands** to shop directly from verified brand stores.

## 6. Managing Wishlist & Shopping Cart

### Saving Items to Wishlist
* Click the **Heart Icon (❤️)** on any item card to save it to your Wishlist for later.
* Access your saved items anytime under **Profile ➔ Wishlist**.

### Adding Items to Shopping Cart
1. Click on a product to view full details, size/quantity options, and seller info.
2. Select your desired quantity.
3. Click **"Add to Cart"**.
4. Click the **Cart Icon 🛒** in the top header to review selected items, total price, and store breakdown.

---

## 7. Checkout & Payment Procedure

Follow these steps to complete your order:

```
[Open Cart] ➔ [Proceed to Checkout] ➔ [Select Address] ➔ [Apply Coupon] ➔ [Select Payment] ➔ [Place Order]
```

### Step 1: Open Cart & Review
Click the **Cart Icon** and verify items and quantities. Click **"Proceed to Checkout"**.

### Step 2: Select Delivery Address
* Choose a saved delivery address (e.g., *Home*, *Work*) OR click **"Add New Address"** to drop a pin on the map and type building details.

### Step 3: Apply Promo Coupon
* If you have a discount code, enter it into the **"Apply Coupon"** field and click **Apply** to reduce your total amount.

### Step 4: Choose Payment Method
Select your preferred payment option:
* 💵 **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Pay cash to the delivery rider upon order arrival.
* 💳 **Digital Card Payment**: Pay securely using Credit/Debit card.
* 👛 **Marketplace Wallet**: Pay instantly using your prepaid wallet balance.

### Step 5: Confirm Order
Review total items, delivery fee, and final bill amount, then click **"Place Order"**.

## 8. Real-Time Order Tracking Procedure

After placing an order, track its status live:

1. Click **"Track Order"** in the top navigation bar or go to **My Account ➔ My Orders**.
2. Select your active Order ID.
3. View real-time status updates:

```
🟡 Order Placed ➔ 🔵 Store Accepted ➔ 🟣 Preparing Items ➔ 🚴 Out for Delivery ➔ 🟢 Delivered
```

* When the rider is assigned, you will see the **Rider's Name**, **Phone Number**, and **Live Map Tracking**.

---

## 9. Special Feature: Sending Parcels

Need to send a document, key, or package across town? Use the **Parcel Delivery** module:

1. Click the **Parcel** module on the homepage.
2. Select **Parcel Category** (e.g., *Document*, *Electronics*, *Clothing*, *Food Package*).
3. Set **Pick-up Location** (Sender Name, Phone, Address).
4. Set **Drop-off Location** (Receiver Name, Phone, Address).
5. Enter estimated package weight.
6. Select Payment Method (Paid by Sender or Paid by Receiver on COD).
7. Click **Confirm Parcel Booking**. A delivery rider will arrive to collect the parcel.

---

## 10. Special Feature: Rental Bookings

To book a vehicle or ride service:

1. Click the **Rental** module on the homepage.
2. Select vehicle type (Car, SUV, Bike, Transport).
3. Set **Pick-up Date & Time** and **Return Date & Time**.
4. Select Pick-up and Drop-off locations.
5. Review pricing and click **Book Ride**.

## 11. Wallet, Loyalty Points & Discount Coupons

### GIFT Wallet
* Access your wallet via **My Account ➔ Wallet**.
* Top up your wallet balance to enjoy 1-click instant checkouts without needing cash.

### Loyalty Points
* Earn reward points on every completed purchase.
* Convert accumulated points into wallet cash under **My Account ➔ Loyalty Points** to apply as instant discounts on future orders.

---

## 12. Order History, Reviews & Customer Support

### Order History & Reordering
* View all past purchases under **My Account ➔ Order History**.
* Click **"Reorder"** to instantly add items from a previous order into your cart.

### Rating & Reviewing
* Once an order is delivered, go to **Rate & Review**.
* Rate the Store/Items and Delivery Rider from 1 to 5 Stars and leave comments.

### Customer Support & Help Center
If you experience any issues (delayed delivery, damaged items, incorrect order):
1. Click **"Help & Support"** in the header navigation.
2. Select your Order ID and choose the issue type.
3. Initiate a live chat with Customer Care or submit a ticket.

* **Email Support**: support@giftmarketplace.com
